How it works

  1. Data. Every NFL game since 1999 with final scores and closing moneylines, spreads and totals, from the open nflverse dataset (updated during the season). It's downloaded fresh each time the page loads.
  2. Team stats. For each team it tracks an Elo rating (adjusted for margin of victory), rolling points scored/allowed, how often they beat the spread, and how often their games go over the total. Recent games count most.
  3. Learning week to week. Three logistic-regression models (win, cover, over) are trained online. Week by week, they predict that week's games, see the results, and update their weights. Then they move on to the next week. When new results are posted, the next page load learns from them.
  4. Finding value. The model's probability is compared with the probability implied by the odds. A bet is only recommended when its expected value is above your minimum edge. Suggested stakes use quarter-Kelly, capped at 3% of bankroll.
