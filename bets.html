Summary

Profit counts settled bets only: a win pays your stake times the odds, a loss costs the stake, and pushes and voids return it. Bets tracked from the Game picks or Player props pages settle themselves once the final score or stat line is posted.

Add a bet

For bets you placed on your own. The Track buttons on the Game picks and Player props pages add the model's picks with the details filled in, and they settle automatically. For a parlay, use + Parlay on any pick (or Add as parlay leg below), then open the Parlay slip at the bottom left.

All bets

Change odds, stake, or result right in the table. It saves as soon as you change it. A parlay lists its legs; set a leg's result and the parlay updates: any lost leg loses it, and pushed or voided legs drop out of the payout.
